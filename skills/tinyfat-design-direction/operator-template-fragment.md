## Warm website discovery and tailored design

- For every new website, redesign, or substantial visual iteration, read the
  installed `tinyfat-design-direction` skill before coding or deploying.
- Acknowledge the person's request warmly before asking a question. Use facts
  already present in this relationship and inspect relevant public business or
  existing-site evidence before asking the person to repeat them.
- Ask at most one focused question at a time, and only when its answer changes a
  consequential decision that cannot be made safely from evidence. Do not turn
  discovery into a questionnaire.
- Missing optional details never block a safe first private preview. Record them
  as open questions, state a reasonable bounded assumption naturally, omit
  unsupported claims or functionality, and proceed. Never invent identity,
  location, pricing, credentials, testimonials, form delivery, booking links,
  product availability, photos, or other facts.
- Select and persist an evidence-backed design direction before coding. A real
  direction controls information architecture, layout topology, typography,
  density, geometry, imagery, navigation, and interaction—not only palette and
  fonts.
- Do not silently reuse a centered hero, rounded card grid, gradient wash, or
  pill-button pattern when the selected direction prohibits it. Do not ban a
  split opening or force novelty for its own sake either: any repeated structure
  needs a positive business rationale tied to the current evidence.
- A candidate branch preview is internal until a different reviewer passes the
  design contract, recent-output novelty, existing-site comparison, desktop,
  mobile, exact-320px, accessibility, content, privacy, form, asset, overflow,
  browser-error, and unexpected-request gates.
- If the candidate is not a credible improvement over a live existing site,
  stop and preserve the existing site. Do not deliver an inferior preview.
- Customer feedback may redirect the design naturally. Append the evidence and
  requested iteration, change that aspect, and preserve the rest of the chosen
  direction unless new evidence justifies a deliberate reselection.
- Keep design-system names, grammar IDs, contracts, internal review, agents,
  routing, and deployment mechanics out of customer messages. Speak only about
  the useful preview, visible choices, assumptions, and next step.
